You Command. They Decide.

You did not tell every agent what to do. You gave the system objectives and constraints.

The agents had different priorities. They disagreed. They communicated. Their recommendations were reconciled.

The result was a collective decision that was not simply the command of any one agent.

That's the basic idea behind multi-agent AI systems.
